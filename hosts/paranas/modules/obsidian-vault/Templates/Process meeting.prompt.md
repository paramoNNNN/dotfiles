# Process meeting note

Preserve the `Raw notes` section exactly as written. Fill the remaining
sections using only facts explicitly present in the raw notes and relevant
linked project notes.

- Write a concise summary.
- Extract explicit decisions.
- Turn commitments into Markdown tasks.
- Include an owner and due date only when explicitly stated.
- Put missing owners or dates under `Open questions`; do not invent them.
- Suggest relevant Obsidian links under `Related`.
- Do not move, rename, or delete any note.
